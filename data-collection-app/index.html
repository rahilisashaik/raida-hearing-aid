Your audiogram

These are the softest levels you heard at each test frequency—plotted like a clinical audiogram (higher = more hearing loss).

Gain profile — reference: minimum

Gain is computed from how much worse each frequency is than the best threshold across your test (after compression).

Gain profile — reference: median (500–2000 Hz)

The baseline uses the median of your 500, 1000, and 2000 Hz thresholds—robust to outliers in the mids.

Gain profile — reference: PTA4

PTA4 averages 500–4000 Hz, similar to common speech-weighted summaries.

Smoothed gain profile

A 3-point moving average across frequency makes the prescription smoother—closer to what a real hearing-aid fitting targets.
